The healthcare and social services occupations category covers a broad NOC list
including specialists in clinical and laboratory medicine (31100), specialists in
surgery (31101), general practitioners and family physicians (31102),
veterinarians (31103), registered nurses, physiotherapists, occupational
therapists, pharmacists, psychologists, social workers and other health and
social service occupations.

Eligibility follows the standard category rule: at least 12 months of full-time
experience, not necessarily continuous, in the past 3 years, in a single listed
occupation, in Canada or abroad.

This category draws frequently and its cutoffs have generally run well below
general-round scores, which is why a nurse with a mid-400s CRS score can be
invited while a software engineer with the same score waits.

To be eligible for an invitation in a category-based round you must first meet
the minimum criteria for Express Entry, including being eligible for one of the
three programs it covers. Category eligibility is checked on top of that, never
instead of it.
