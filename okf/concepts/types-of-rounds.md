There are three kinds of rounds of invitations, and which one is running decides
who can be invited:

- General rounds invite the top-ranked candidates from the whole pool, across all
  three programs. These have been rare since 2024.
- Program-specific rounds invite only candidates eligible for one program — a
  Canadian Experience Class round, for example, invites only CEC-eligible
  candidates.
- Category-based rounds invite only candidates who meet a category's
  requirements, on top of being eligible for one of the three programs.

Provincial nominee program rounds are a program-specific type in practice: they
invite candidates holding an enhanced provincial nomination, whose 600 additional
points put them far above everyone else, which is why PNP cutoffs are the highest
published and mean the least.

IRCC may skip a category round entirely if enough top-ranking candidates eligible
for that category are already being invited through general or program-specific
rounds.
