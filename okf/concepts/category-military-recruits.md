The skilled military recruits category works differently from every other
category. To be eligible you must be a Foreign Skilled Military Applicant: a
foreign national serving in a recognised foreign military with a minimum of ten
years of continuous service, whose experience and training align with the main
duties of NOC 40042 (commissioned officers of the Canadian Armed Forces), 42102
(specialized members) or 43204 (operations members).

You must also:

- hold an offer of arranged employment with the Canadian Armed Forces, made by
  the Canadian Forces Recruiting Group, for continuous full-time work in Canada
  lasting at least 3 years, in one of those three NOC codes; and
- have at least a 2-year post-secondary credential, with an educational
  credential assessment if it was earned outside Canada.

Rounds in this category are very small — single-digit or low double-digit
invitation counts — and their cutoffs are correspondingly low.

To be eligible for an invitation in a category-based round you must first meet
the minimum criteria for Express Entry, including being eligible for one of the
three programs it covers. Category eligibility is checked on top of that, never
instead of it.
