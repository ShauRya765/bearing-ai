IRCC invites candidates from the Express Entry pool in rounds held throughout the
year. Each round sets a number of invitations, ranks the eligible candidates by
CRS score, and invites from the top down. The score of the lowest-ranked
candidate invited becomes that round's published cutoff.

The cutoff is therefore an outcome, not a threshold set in advance. It moves with
who is in the pool and how many invitations are issued: a larger round reaches
further down and produces a lower cutoff.

A published cutoff only tells you something about candidates who were eligible
for that round. Comparing your score against the cutoff of a round you could not
have been invited from — a category you do not qualify for, or a program you are
not eligible under — tells you nothing at all.

If you receive an invitation to apply, you have 60 days to submit a complete
application for permanent residence.
