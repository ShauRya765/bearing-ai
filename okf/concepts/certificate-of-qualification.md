A certificate of qualification is issued by a Canadian province, territory, or
a federal body after they assess your training and experience in a skilled
trade and you pass a certification exam. It is not a job offer and not a
provincial nomination — it certifies that you are qualified to practise a
specific trade in Canada.

Under the skill-transferability factor it is worth:

- 25 points combined with a first official language of CLB 5 or higher (but
  below CLB 7).
- 50 points combined with a first official language of CLB 7 or higher.

These points fall under skill transferability, which is capped at 100 points
in total, so the certificate combines with — rather than stacks on top of —
your other transferability points.
