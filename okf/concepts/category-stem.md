The STEM occupations category covers occupations such as architecture and science
managers (20011), cybersecurity specialists (21220), data scientists, software
engineers and designers, computer engineers, mathematicians and statisticians,
civil, mechanical and electrical engineers, and land surveyors.

Eligibility follows the standard category rule: 12 months of full-time experience
in the past 3 years, not necessarily continuous, in a single listed occupation,
in Canada or abroad.

Being a software developer is not automatically enough. The category is defined
by NOC code, and several common technology job titles sit in codes that are not
on the list. Check the code against the published list rather than reasoning from
the job title.

To be eligible for an invitation in a category-based round you must first meet
the minimum criteria for Express Entry, including being eligible for one of the
three programs it covers. Category eligibility is checked on top of that, never
instead of it.
