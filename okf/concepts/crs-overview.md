The Comprehensive Ranking System scores a profile out of 1,200 points across four
sections:

- A. Core / human capital factors — age, level of education, official language
  proficiency, and Canadian work experience. Maximum 500 points without a spouse
  or common-law partner, 460 with one.
- B. Spouse or common-law partner factors — their education, language and
  Canadian work experience. Maximum 40 points, and only when a spouse is coming
  with you.
- C. Skill transferability factors — combinations of education, foreign work
  experience, a certificate of qualification and language. Maximum 100 points.
- D. Additional points — provincial nomination, French-language skills, a
  sibling in Canada, and post-secondary education in Canada. Maximum 600 points.

Sections A, B and C together are capped at 600, and section D is capped at 600,
which is where the 1,200 total comes from. Each section has its own internal
maximums as well, so hitting the top of one factor does not carry over into
another.

Note that the with-spouse and without-spouse columns are different throughout
sections A and C. Points do not simply split between two people.
