The education occupations category covers occupations such as elementary school
and kindergarten teachers, secondary school teachers, early childhood educators
and assistants, instructors of persons with disabilities, and elementary and
secondary school teacher assistants.

Eligibility follows the standard category rule: 12 months of full-time experience
in the past 3 years, not necessarily continuous, in a single listed occupation,
in Canada or abroad.

Being eligible for a category is not the same as that category drawing. IRCC
holds category rounds when they serve the economic goal behind the category, and
a category can go many months without a round.

To be eligible for an invitation in a category-based round you must first meet
the minimum criteria for Express Entry, including being eligible for one of the
three programs it covers. Category eligibility is checked on top of that, never
instead of it.
