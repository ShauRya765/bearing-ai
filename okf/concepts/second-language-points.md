If you take approved tests in both English and French, your second (weaker)
official language earns extra CRS points, capped at 24 points in total. Points
are awarded per ability — reading, writing, listening, speaking — never
averaged:

- CLB/NCLC 9 or higher: 6 points per ability.
- CLB/NCLC 7 or 8: 3 points per ability.
- CLB/NCLC 5 or 6: 1 point per ability.
- Below CLB/NCLC 5: 0 points.

Most applicants test in only one official language and score 0 here. A strong
French second language can also trigger the separate French-language bonus
(up to 50 additional points), so testing in French is usually worth far more
than these 24 points alone.
