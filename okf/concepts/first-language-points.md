Your first (strongest) official language is scored per ability — reading,
writing, listening, speaking — and never averaged. For a single applicant,
points per ability rise sharply with each level (slightly lower if you have an
accompanying spouse):

- CLB 6: 9 points per ability
- CLB 7: 17 points per ability
- CLB 8: 23 points per ability
- CLB 9: 31 points per ability
- CLB 10 or higher: 34 points per ability (up to 136 across all four)

Reaching CLB 9 in every ability matters twice: it adds points directly, and it
unlocks the highest tier of skill-transferability points, which combine
language with education and work experience. Because a single ability below
CLB 9 holds back both, raising the weakest ability is often the single most
effective way to increase a CRS score.
