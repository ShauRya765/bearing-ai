You get 15 additional CRS points for having a brother or sister living in Canada
who is 18 years or older and a Canadian citizen or permanent resident.

Each condition is load-bearing:

- Sibling specifically. Cousins, aunts, uncles, grandparents and in-laws do not
  qualify for these points, however close the relationship.
- Living in Canada. A Canadian citizen sibling living abroad does not count.
- 18 or older.
- A citizen or permanent resident. A sibling in Canada on a work or study permit
  does not count.

A sibling of your spouse or common-law partner counts, provided the spouse is
coming with you, since the relationship is assessed for either of you.

The 15 points are claimed in the profile and have to be documented if you are
invited.
