You enter the pool by creating an Express Entry profile in an IRCC secure
account. The profile is where you declare your age, education, language test
results, work history, family situation and any provincial nomination.

If the profile shows you are eligible for at least one of the three programs —
federal skilled worker class, federal skilled trades class or Canadian experience
class — IRCC places you in the pool and assigns you a CRS score, visible in your
account. If it shows you are eligible for none of them, you are not placed in the
pool and there is no score.

IRCC identifies which candidates are eligible for a given category from what the
profile says. Category eligibility is not something you apply for separately; it
is derived from your declared work experience and language results.

A profile is a set of declarations, not evidence. Documents are only assessed
after an invitation to apply, which is why an inaccurate profile can produce a
CRS score, an invitation, and then a refusal.
