Canadian work experience is a core human-capital factor worth up to 80 points
without a spouse or common-law partner, and up to 70 with one.

Without a spouse (with a spouse in brackets):

- None, or less than a year: 0 (0).
- 1 year: 40 (35).
- 2 years: 53 (46).
- 3 years: 64 (56).
- 4 years: 72 (63).
- 5 years or more: 80 (70).

Half the value arrives with the first year, and the increments shrink after
that — the fifth year is worth 8 points where the first was worth 40.

Canadian work experience also pays a second time through the skill
transferability factors, where it combines with education and with foreign work
experience for up to 50 points each. A candidate weighing a year in Canada
against a year abroad is not comparing like with like: foreign work experience
earns no core points at all.
