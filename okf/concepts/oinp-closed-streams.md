Ontario redesigned the OINP in June 2026. Changes to Ontario Regulation 422/17
under the Ontario Immigration Act, 2015, made on June 25 and 26, 2026, removed
eight streams and introduced the Ontario Workforce Priority stream in their
place.

The eight closed streams are Employer Job Offer: Foreign Worker, Employer Job
Offer: In-Demand Skills, Employer Job Offer: International Student, Master's
Graduate, PhD Graduate, Express Entry Human Capital Priorities, Express Entry
French-Speaking Skilled Worker, and Express Entry Skilled Trades.

None of them accept applications. The expression of interest system that
served them is closed to new registrations, and no further invitations will be
issued under any of them. Guidance that tells a candidate to apply through one
of these streams — including the Human Capital Priorities notification of
interest route that Ontario used to issue from the Express Entry pool — is out
of date. The Ontario Workforce Priority stream is the only route into an
Ontario nomination for skilled workers.
