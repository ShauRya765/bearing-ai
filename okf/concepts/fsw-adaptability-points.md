Adaptability is worth a maximum of 10 points in the Federal Skilled Worker
selection-factor grid. You and an accompanying spouse or common-law partner can
combine any of these elements up to that ceiling:

- Your past work in Canada — at least 1 year of full-time work in a TEER 0, 1, 2
  or 3 job, with a valid work permit or while authorised to work: 10 points.
- Your past studies in Canada — at least 2 academic years of full-time study in a
  program at least 2 years long, in good academic standing: 5 points.
- Your spouse's past studies in Canada, on the same terms: 5 points.
- Your spouse's past work in Canada — at least 1 year full-time, authorised: 5
  points.
- Your spouse's language level — CLB 4 or higher in all four abilities: 5 points.
- Arranged employment in Canada, where you earned the arranged-employment points:
  5 points.
- Relatives in Canada — you or your spouse have a relative living in Canada who
  is 18 or older and a Canadian citizen or permanent resident: 5 points.

Full-time study means at least 15 hours of classes per week. The relatives
element here is broader than the CRS sibling factor, which counts only a brother
or sister.
