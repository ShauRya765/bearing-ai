Strong French means NCLC 7 or higher (equivalent to CLB 7) in all four
abilities on an approved French test (TEF Canada or TCF Canada). It awards
additional CRS points on top of the points French already earns as a first or
second official language:

- 25 points if your English is CLB 4 or lower, or you have no English test.
- 50 points if your English is CLB 5 or higher in all four abilities.

Strong French (NCLC 7+) also makes you eligible for the French-language
proficiency category. Draws in this category happen frequently and have often
had lower cut-off scores than general draws, so targeting French is one of the
most reliable ways to improve the odds of an invitation.
