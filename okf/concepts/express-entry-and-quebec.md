Express Entry does not apply to Quebec. The province selects its own economic
immigrants under the Canada–Quebec Accord, through its own programs, and a
candidate who intends to settle in Quebec cannot be selected through Express
Entry to do so.

Two practical consequences:

- Express Entry candidates declare an intended province of residence other than
  Quebec. Permanent residence is not conditional on staying there — mobility
  rights apply once you are a permanent resident — but the declaration should be
  a genuine intention when you make it.
- Quebec does not have a provincial nominee program stream that plugs into
  Express Entry. The 600 additional points for a provincial or territorial
  nomination cannot come from Quebec.

Work experience gained in Quebec still counts as Canadian work experience for
CRS points and for Canadian Experience Class eligibility. It is the selection
route, not the experience, that Quebec handles separately.
