The researchers with Canadian work experience category covers occupations such as
university professors and lecturers (41200) and post-secondary teaching and
research assistants (41201).

Eligibility requires at least 12 months of full-time experience, not necessarily
continuous, within the past 3 years, in a single listed occupation, in Canada.

Post-doctoral researchers and research assistants employed by Canadian
universities are the intended population. The category does not cover research
work classified under other NOC codes — an industrial R&D scientist is more
likely to fall under the STEM category, which accepts foreign experience.

To be eligible for an invitation in a category-based round you must first meet
the minimum criteria for Express Entry, including being eligible for one of the
three programs it covers. Category eligibility is checked on top of that, never
instead of it.
