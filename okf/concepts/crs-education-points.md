Level of education is worth up to 150 points without a spouse or common-law
partner, and up to 140 with one. Points are given for the highest credential you
have, except for the two-or-more tier.

Without a spouse (with a spouse in brackets):

- Less than secondary school: 0 (0).
- Secondary diploma: 30 (28).
- One-year post-secondary credential: 90 (84).
- Two-year program: 98 (91).
- Bachelor's degree, or a three-or-more-year program: 120 (112).
- Two or more credentials, one of which was for a program of three or more
  years: 128 (119).
- Master's degree, or a professional degree needed to practise a licensed
  profession: 135 (126).
- Doctoral degree (PhD): 150 (140).

A professional degree here means medicine, veterinary medicine, dentistry,
optometry, law, chiropractic medicine or pharmacy specifically.

The two-or-more-credentials tier is the one people miss. It outranks a single
bachelor's degree by 8 points, and someone with a diploma plus a degree qualifies
for it — but only if each credential has its own educational credential
assessment.
