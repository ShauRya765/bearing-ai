The Comprehensive Ranking System awards points for skill transferability,
rewarding combinations of factors rather than any factor alone. Points come
from these pairings:

- Education combined with strong language ability.
- Education combined with Canadian work experience.
- Foreign work experience combined with strong language ability.
- Foreign work experience combined with Canadian work experience.
- A certificate of qualification combined with language ability.

Each pairing pays more at higher thresholds: language at CLB 9+ in all four
abilities pays more than CLB 7–8, and more education or more years of work
moves you into a higher tier. All skill-transferability points are capped at
100 in total, so the pairings combine toward one ceiling rather than stacking
without limit. Because of the cap and the CLB 9 thresholds, raising language
to CLB 9 in every ability often unlocks the largest single increase here.
