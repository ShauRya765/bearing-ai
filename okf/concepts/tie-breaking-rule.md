description: When candidates tie at the cutoff, the earlier profile submission wins.

Rounds of invitations publish a tie-breaking rule alongside the cutoff: a date and
time. If more than one candidate has the lowest score in the round, the cutoff is
based on when they submitted their Express Entry profile. Candidates who
submitted their profile before that timestamp are invited; candidates at the same
score who submitted after it are not.

Two consequences follow.

Submitting a profile early has value even before your score is competitive. Time
in the pool is the tiebreaker, and it only accrues while your profile is in it.

Second, the tiebreaker is the profile submission timestamp, not your score
history. Two candidates who reach the same score by different routes are
separated only by which of them has been in the pool longer.
