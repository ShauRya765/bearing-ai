Every occupation-based category uses the same work-experience test. To be
eligible you must have accumulated, within the past 3 years, at least 12 months
of full-time work experience — or an equal amount of part-time experience — in a
single occupation on that category's NOC list.

Four details in that sentence do the work:

- The experience does not need to be continuous. Twelve months assembled from
  separate stretches inside the three-year window counts.
- It must be in a single occupation from the list. Six months in one listed
  occupation and six in another does not add up to eligibility.
- It counts no matter your primary occupation. The occupation you are using to
  qualify for your Express Entry program can be different from the one that makes
  you category-eligible.
- Location depends on the category. Healthcare, STEM, trades, education and
  transport accept experience in Canada or abroad. The physicians, senior
  managers and researchers categories require the experience to be in Canada.

Full details are set out in the ministerial instructions for each round, and a
round's instructions can be narrower than the general rule.
