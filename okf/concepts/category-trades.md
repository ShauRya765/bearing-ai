The trade occupations category covers occupations including construction
estimators (22303), construction managers, carpenters, plumbers, electricians,
welders, heavy-duty equipment mechanics, machine fitters, crane operators and
contractors and supervisors in the trades.

Eligibility follows the standard category rule: 12 months of full-time experience
in the past 3 years, not necessarily continuous, in a single listed occupation,
in Canada or abroad.

Category eligibility is separate from the Federal Skilled Trades Program. They
use different lists and different tests: FST is an eligibility route into the
pool needing 2 years of trade experience plus a job offer or certificate of
qualification, while the trades category decides whether you can be invited in a
trades round once you are already in the pool through any program.

To be eligible for an invitation in a category-based round you must first meet
the minimum criteria for Express Entry, including being eligible for one of the
three programs it covers. Category eligibility is checked on top of that, never
instead of it.
