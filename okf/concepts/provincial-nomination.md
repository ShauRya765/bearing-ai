A provincial nomination through the Provincial Nominee Program adds 600
points to a candidate's Comprehensive Ranking System score, effectively
guaranteeing an invitation to apply in a subsequent Express Entry round.

There are two routes. An "enhanced" nomination is aligned with Express Entry:
it adds the 600 CRS points and you are invited through an Express Entry draw.
A "base" nomination is outside Express Entry — it does not add CRS points and
follows a separate, usually slower, application path.

To get a nomination, you apply to a specific province or territory's PNP
stream. Each one sets its own streams, in-demand occupations, and criteria,
and many target candidates who plan to live and work in that province. Some
streams require an Express Entry profile and invite you directly from it;
others accept applications on their own. If selected, you receive a nomination
certificate, which you then claim on your Express Entry profile for the 600
points.
