As of March 25, 2025, IRCC removed job offer points from the Comprehensive
Ranking System for all candidates in the Express Entry pool, current and future.
That removed:

- 200 points for a job offer classified under Major Group 00 of the NOC, such as
  senior management positions, and
- 50 points for a job offer in any other skilled occupation.

Nothing replaced them. A valid LMIA-backed job offer is now worth zero CRS points.

What did not change is eligibility. A job offer is still part of the criteria
for:

- the Federal Skilled Trades Program, where you need either a job offer or a
  certificate of qualification;
- the Federal Skilled Worker Program, where arranged employment is worth 10 of
  the 67 selection-factor points; and
- some provincial nominee program streams, several of which are built entirely
  around an employer offer.

You should still enter job offer details in your Express Entry profile so you
stay eligible for those programs, even though the CRS ignores them.
