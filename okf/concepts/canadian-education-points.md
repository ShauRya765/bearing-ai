Studying in Canada earns additional CRS points, separate from and on top of
your education factor:

- 15 points for a 1- or 2-year post-secondary credential.
- 30 points for a credential of 3 years or longer, or a master's,
  professional, or doctoral degree of at least one academic year.

To count, the program must have been full-time, at least 8 months long, and
completed while you were physically studying in Canada — foreign campuses of
Canadian schools do not qualify. English or French as a second language cannot
have made up more than half of the study, and you cannot have been on a
scholarship that required you to return to your home country.
