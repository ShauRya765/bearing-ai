The physicians with Canadian work experience category covers specialists in
clinical and laboratory medicine (31100), specialists in surgery (31101) and
general practitioners and family physicians (31102).

Eligibility requires at least 12 months of full-time experience, not necessarily
continuous, within the past 3 years, in a single listed occupation — and unlike
healthcare, STEM, trades, education and transport, that experience must be in
Canada.

Two gates therefore apply at once: the right occupation and Canadian experience.
A physician with ten years of practice abroad and none in Canada is not eligible
for this category, though they may be eligible for the broader healthcare
category, which accepts foreign experience.

Physicians whose Canadian work is self-employed fee-for-service should read the
public policy that lets that experience count as Canadian work experience.

To be eligible for an invitation in a category-based round you must first meet
the minimum criteria for Express Entry, including being eligible for one of the
three programs it covers. Category eligibility is checked on top of that, never
instead of it.
