Section D of the CRS holds the points that sit outside human capital. The whole
group is capped at 600:

- Provincial or territorial nomination — 600.
- French-language skills — 25 or 50.
- Post-secondary education in Canada — 15 for a one- or two-year credential, 30
  for three years or longer.
- A brother or sister living in Canada who is 18 or older and a citizen or
  permanent resident — 15.

The 600-point cap is not decorative. A nominated candidate is already at the
ceiling, so a sibling, French, and a Canadian credential add nothing on top of a
nomination. For everyone else the group tops out at 95 points, which is still
more than the gap between most candidates and a category cutoff.

Job offer points used to live in this section. They were removed on March 25,
2025 and no longer appear anywhere in the CRS.
