Selection for the Ontario Workforce Priority stream runs on an expression of
interest, not a ranked points draw, and the employer starts it. The employer
submits the job offer through the employer portal; the candidate then
registers an expression of interest within 30 calendar days; and the OINP
issues invitations to apply from the registrations it holds.

Every step after an invitation is on a short clock. The invited employer has
14 calendar days to submit its part of the application, and the applicant has
17 calendar days to submit theirs once the employer has done so.

After a nomination is issued, an Express Entry candidate has 30 calendar days
to accept or decline it in their Express Entry profile, and 60 calendar days
to submit the permanent residence application to IRCC. A base nominee, outside
Express Entry, has six months to apply to IRCC.
