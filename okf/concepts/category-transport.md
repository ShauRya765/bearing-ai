The transport occupations category covers occupations such as aircraft mechanics
and aircraft inspectors, air traffic controllers, railway traffic controllers,
engineer officers in water transport, deck officers in water transport, managers
in transportation, and transport truck drivers in some instruction versions.

Eligibility follows the standard category rule: 12 months of full-time experience
in the past 3 years, not necessarily continuous, in a single listed occupation,
in Canada or abroad.

The published NOC list for this category has changed between instruction
versions, so a candidate who was eligible under an earlier version is not
automatically eligible under the current one.

To be eligible for an invitation in a category-based round you must first meet
the minimum criteria for Express Entry, including being eligible for one of the
three programs it covers. Category eligibility is checked on top of that, never
instead of it.
