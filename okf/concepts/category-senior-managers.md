The senior managers with Canadian work experience category covers Major Group 00
occupations: legislators (00010), senior government managers and officials
(00011), senior managers in financial, communications and other business services
(00012), in health, education, social and community services and membership
organisations (00013), in trade, broadcasting and other services (00014), and in
construction, transportation, production and utilities (00015).

Eligibility requires at least 12 months of full-time experience, not necessarily
continuous, within the past 3 years, in a single listed occupation, in Canada.

There is an irony worth knowing. Major Group 00 job offers used to be worth 200
CRS points, the largest single award outside a provincial nomination, until job
offer points were removed on March 25, 2025. Senior management now matters
through category eligibility rather than through points.

To be eligible for an invitation in a category-based round you must first meet
the minimum criteria for Express Entry, including being eligible for one of the
three programs it covers. Category eligibility is checked on top of that, never
instead of it.
