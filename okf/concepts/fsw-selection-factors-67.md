The Federal Skilled Worker Program scores you out of 100 on six selection
factors. Score 67 or higher and you may qualify for the program; score lower and
you do not, regardless of your CRS score. These points are entirely separate from
the Comprehensive Ranking System that ranks profiles in the pool.

- Language skills — maximum 28. First official language 24, at 4/5/6 points per
  ability for CLB 7/8/9 or higher. Below CLB 7 in any ability makes you
  ineligible to apply. Second official language 4 points, all or nothing, and
  only at CLB 5 or better in all four abilities.
- Education — maximum 25.
- Skilled work experience — maximum 15. 1 year 9, 2–3 years 11, 4–5 years 13,
  6 or more years 15.
- Age — maximum 12. 18 to 35 gets the full 12, then one point falls away per
  year: 36 gets 11, 40 gets 7, 46 gets 1, and 47 or older gets 0.
- Arranged employment in Canada — maximum 10.
- Adaptability — maximum 10.

The age curve here is much flatter than the CRS one, and it runs to 47 rather
than 45. Two different age tables for the same candidate is normal; they answer
different questions.
