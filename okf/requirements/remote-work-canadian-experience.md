Canadian work experience means working for a Canadian employer, in Canada.
Remote work counts, but only if you were physically present in Canada while
doing it.

This rules out two common situations that feel Canadian but are not:

- Working remotely for a Canadian company from another country. The employer is
  Canadian, but you were not in Canada, so the experience is foreign work
  experience.
- Working in Canada for a foreign employer with no Canadian presence. You were
  in Canada, but the employer is not a Canadian employer.

The distinction matters twice over. Canadian work experience is a core CRS
factor worth up to 80 points on its own, and it is also what the Canadian
Experience Class is built on. Foreign work experience earns no core points at
all and only pays through the skill transferability factors.

For the Canadian Experience Class the work must also have been done under
temporary resident status with authorization to work.
