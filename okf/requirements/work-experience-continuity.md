The minimum is stated two ways: 1 year of continuous work, or 1,560 hours in
total. Meeting either one satisfies the requirement, which is why part-time work
across several jobs can add up to a qualifying year.

The window in which that year must fall differs by program:

- Federal Skilled Worker — within the last 10 years.
- Federal Skilled Trades — within the 5 years before you apply, and the
  requirement is 2 years or 3,120 hours rather than one year.
- Canadian Experience Class — within the 3 years before you apply.

The Canadian Experience Class window is the one that expires on people. Canadian
experience gained more than three years before you apply stops counting for CEC
eligibility, even though it may still earn Comprehensive Ranking System points
for Canadian work experience.
