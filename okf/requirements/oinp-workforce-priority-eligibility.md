The Ontario Workforce Priority stream sets different thresholds for higher and
lower NOC TEER bands. These are Ontario's own provincial requirements and are
lower than the federal Express Entry program minimums; they do not replace
them.

For a job offer in TEER 0, 1, 2, or 3, the applicant needs work experience
meeting one of: six months of consecutive paid full-time experience in the job
offer's position within the 12 months before applying; three months for a
recent graduate of an eligible Ontario institution; two years of cumulative
paid full-time experience in the same NOC occupation within the past five
years; or a valid mandatory licence for the position. Education must be a
postsecondary credential taking at least one year of full-time study, either
Canadian or foreign with an Educational Credential Assessment, though a high
school diploma suffices for skilled trades and a one-year Ontario College
Certificate for nursing assistants. Language must be CLB 6 or higher in all
four abilities, or CLB 5 for skilled trades, unless the applicant graduated
from an eligible Ontario institution within the last three years.

For a job offer in TEER 4 or 5, the applicant needs nine months of cumulative
paid full-time work experience within the two years before applying, a
Canadian secondary school diploma or its equivalent, and CLB 4 or higher in
all four abilities.

An Educational Credential Assessment report must be less than five years old
when the application is submitted. Language results must come from a test
taken within the two years before the application date; the stream accepts
IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General, and PTE Core for English, and TEF
Canada or TCF Canada for French.
