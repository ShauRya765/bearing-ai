IRCC uses the 2021 National Occupational Classification to identify and
categorise jobs by the training, education, experience and responsibilities
(TEER) they require. The switch to NOC 2021 happened on November 16, 2022.

- TEER 0 — management occupations. Example: financial managers.
- TEER 1 — occupations that usually require a university degree. Examples:
  financial advisors, software engineers.
- TEER 2 — occupations that usually require a college diploma, apprenticeship
  training of 2 or more years, or supervisory responsibility. Examples: computer
  network technicians, medical laboratory technologists.
- TEER 3 — occupations that usually require a college diploma, apprenticeship
  training of less than 2 years, or more than 6 months of on-the-job training.
  Examples: bakers, dental assistants.
- TEER 4 — occupations that usually require a high school diploma or several
  weeks of on-the-job training. Examples: home child care providers, retail
  salespersons.
- TEER 5 — occupations needing short-term work demonstration and no formal
  education. Examples: landscaping labourers, delivery drivers.

Every NOC has a five-digit code. The second digit is the TEER category, so the
code itself tells you the band.
