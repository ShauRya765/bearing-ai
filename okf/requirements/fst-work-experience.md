The Federal Skilled Trades Program requires at least 2 years of full-time work
experience, or 3,120 hours in total, in a skilled trade within the 5 years before
you apply.

You can reach 3,120 hours as full-time work at one job (30 hours a week for 24
months), an equivalent amount of part-time work (for example 15 hours a week for
48 months), or full-time work across more than one job. Hours worked above 30 a
week are not counted.

Two conditions catch people out:

- The experience must have been obtained in a country where you were qualified
  to practise that trade. If you qualified in one country and worked in another
  with different rules, you must also have met the requirements to practise
  independently there.
- Work experience gained while you were studying does not count toward the
  minimum requirements for this program at all — unlike the Federal Skilled
  Worker Program, where paid, continuous student work can count.
