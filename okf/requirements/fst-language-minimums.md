The Federal Skilled Trades Program sets different minimums per ability, and they
are the lowest of any Express Entry program:

- English: CLB 5 in speaking and listening, CLB 4 in reading and writing.
- French: NCLC 5 in speaking and listening, NCLC 4 in reading and writing.

The split is deliberate. Trades work is spoken and heard on a site more than it
is read and written, so the program asks for more of the oral abilities.

Meeting this floor gets you into the pool through FST. It does not earn many
Comprehensive Ranking System points — the CRS language table pays little below
CLB 7 — so a tradesperson who qualifies at CLB 4/5 will usually rank low unless
they also hold a certificate of qualification or a provincial nomination.
