The Canadian Experience Class does not have a single language floor. The minimum
depends on the TEER category of the job you are using:

- TEER 0 or TEER 1 — CLB 7 in English, or NCLC 7 in French, in all four
  abilities.
- TEER 2 or TEER 3 — CLB 5 in English, or NCLC 5 in French, in all four
  abilities.

This is the most common source of confusion with the federal CLB 7 figure. CLB 7
is the Federal Skilled Worker minimum for everyone, and the CEC minimum only for
TEER 0 and 1 jobs. A candidate with a TEER 2 or TEER 3 Canadian job can be
eligible for the Canadian Experience Class at CLB 5.

The floor applies per ability. The lowest of your four scores decides whether you
meet it — you cannot average them.
