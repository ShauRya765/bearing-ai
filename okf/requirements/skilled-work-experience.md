Skilled work experience is the backbone of Express Entry eligibility. To count,
your experience must:

- be in TEER category 0, 1, 2 or 3 of the National Occupational Classification;
- be in the same NOC as your primary occupation (for FSW and FST);
- show that you performed the actions in the lead statement of the NOC job
  description, and most of the main duties listed;
- be paid work — you must have been paid wages or earned commission. Volunteer
  work and unpaid internships do not count;
- total at least 1 year of continuous work, or 1,560 hours.

Work in a TEER 4 or TEER 5 occupation does not count as skilled work experience
for any of the three Express Entry programs, no matter how long you did it.

The look-back window depends on the program: the last 10 years for Federal
Skilled Worker, the last 3 years for Canadian Experience Class, and the last 5
years for Federal Skilled Trades.
