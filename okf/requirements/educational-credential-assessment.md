An educational credential assessment (ECA) verifies that a foreign degree,
diploma or certificate is valid and equal to a Canadian one. It must be an ECA
for immigration purposes — other types do not count, though some can be
re-issued.

You need an ECA if you completed your education outside Canada and you want to:

- be eligible as the principal applicant for the Federal Skilled Worker Program,
  or
- earn CRS points for education you got outside Canada.

You do not need an assessment for a Canadian degree, diploma or certificate. You
can also get one for your spouse or common-law partner's foreign education if
they are coming with you.

Your ECA must be less than 5 years old both when you complete your Express Entry
profile and when you submit your application. Applying with an expired ECA gets
the application refused.

In most cases you only need your highest level of education assessed. The
assessment must be done by an organization designated by IRCC — for example
World Education Services, the Comparative Education Service at the University of
Toronto, or the International Credential Assessment Service of Canada.
