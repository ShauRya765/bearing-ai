Every Federal Skilled Trades applicant must have one of these two things:

- a valid job offer of full-time employment for a total period of at least 1
  year, or
- a certificate of qualification in the skilled trade, issued by a Canadian
  provincial, territorial or federal authority.

A certificate of qualification proves you are qualified to practise the trade in
Canada: you passed a certification exam and you meet the requirements to practise
in the issuing province or territory. Getting one usually means being assessed by
that province's trades body, which may require travelling there, and in some
cases needing a Canadian employer to provide experience and training first.

If the province or territory where you plan to settle does not issue certificates
of qualification in your trade, the job offer route is your only option for this
program.

Note that this job offer is an eligibility requirement, and is separate from CRS
points. Job offer points were removed from the Comprehensive Ranking System on
March 25, 2025, but job offers still matter for FST eligibility.
