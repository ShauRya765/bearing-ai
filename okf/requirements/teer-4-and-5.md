Experience in a TEER 4 or TEER 5 occupation does not count as skilled work
experience for any Express Entry program. Federal Skilled Worker and Canadian
Experience Class both require TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3, and the Federal Skilled Trades
Program requires specific trade NOC groups.

This is a hard eligibility line, not a scoring penalty. A candidate with five
years in a TEER 4 job and excellent language scores is not a low-scoring
candidate — they are not in the pool at all unless some other experience or
program route qualifies them.

Routes that can still work:

- A provincial nominee program stream that targets the occupation. Provinces set
  their own TEER rules and some accept TEER 4 and 5 jobs.
- Different experience in a TEER 0–3 occupation, if you have any within the
  program's look-back window.
- Qualifying through the Federal Skilled Trades Program, whose eligible NOC
  groups include some occupations outside TEER 0–3.
