Your primary occupation is the job you use for your immigration application. It
is the NOC you are assessed against, and it may not be your most recent work
experience.

Choose the NOC that most closely aligns with what you actually did. The test is
the job description, not the job title: you must have performed the actions in
the lead statement and most of the main duties listed under that NOC. If the
duties do not match, the code is wrong even if the title on your contract is
identical.

Choosing accurately avoids processing delays and makes sure you are assessed
against the correct program.

For the Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades programs, the
qualifying experience must all be in the same NOC. The Canadian Experience Class
is more forgiving — your Canadian experience can be spread across more than one
NOC, as long as each is TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3.
