You must prove your language ability with a test from an agency approved by
IRCC. Results from any other test, or from an unapproved version of an approved
test, cannot be entered in an Express Entry profile.

English:

- CELPIP — you must take the CELPIP-General test.
- IELTS — you must take the IELTS General Training option. IELTS One Skill
  Retake is not accepted for Express Entry.
- PTE Core — you must take the PTE Core option.

French:

- TEF Canada.
- TCF Canada.

English scores are expressed as Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB) and French
scores as Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadiens (NCLC). Every program
minimum and every CRS language figure is stated in those units, so a raw test
score has to be converted before it means anything.

If a disability prevents you from completing one or more sections, IRCC's
language averaging tool produces averaged scores for the abilities you could not
complete, and those are what you enter in the profile.
