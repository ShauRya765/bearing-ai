The treatment of self-employment and student work is not uniform across Express
Entry, which is why general advice about it is usually wrong for someone.

Canadian Experience Class: self-employment does not count toward the minimum
requirements, and neither does work experience gained while you were a full-time
student — including a co-op work term.

Federal Skilled Worker: work experience gained while you were studying may count
toward the minimum requirements, provided it was paid by wages or commissions,
was continuous with no gaps in employment, and meets all the other requirements
of the program. Self-employed experience counts toward FSW selection-factor
points.

Federal Skilled Trades: work experience gained while you were studying does not
count toward the minimum requirements.

The common thread is that the experience must be paid. Volunteer work and unpaid
internships never count toward the minimum for any of the three programs.
