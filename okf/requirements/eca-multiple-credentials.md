The CRS education table has a tier for two or more certificates, diplomas or
degrees, where at least one was for a program of three or more years. It is worth
119 points with a spouse and 128 without — more than a bachelor's degree alone.

To claim it with foreign education, you need an educational credential assessment
for each credential, not just your highest one. This is the exception to the
usual advice that only your highest credential needs assessing.

To get full points for more than one credential, at least one of them must be for
three or more years of study. The order in which you completed them does not
affect the points.

There is a second, less obvious use for a spare assessment. If your post-secondary
credential turns out not to be equal to a Canadian post-secondary credential, you
can have your secondary credential assessed instead, which at least earns the
points for completing secondary school rather than zero.
