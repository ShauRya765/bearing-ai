The Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSW) is for skilled workers with foreign
work experience who want to become permanent residents. It is one of the three
programs Express Entry manages.

To be eligible you must meet all of the minimum requirements:

- Skilled work experience in TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3, in the same NOC as your primary
  occupation, obtained within the last 10 years, in Canada or abroad.
- At least 1 year of continuous work, or 1,560 hours in total, of paid work.
- Canadian Language Benchmark 7 in all four abilities in your first official
  language.
- A completed credential — either Canadian, or foreign with an educational
  credential assessment.

Meeting the minimum requirements is not enough on its own. FSW also scores you
out of 100 on six selection factors, and you need 67 points or more to qualify.
Those selection-factor points are a separate calculation from the Comprehensive
Ranking System score that ranks you in the pool.
