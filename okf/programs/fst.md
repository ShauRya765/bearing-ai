The Federal Skilled Trades Program (FST) is for skilled workers who want
permanent residence on the strength of being qualified in a skilled trade.
Trades sit in sectors such as construction, transportation, manufacturing and
industrial work, and natural resources and agriculture.

Requirements:

- At least 2 years of full-time work experience, or 3,120 hours, in a skilled
  trade within the 5 years before you apply.
- All the experience must be in the same NOC, drawn from Major Groups 72
  (excluding Sub-Major Group 726), 73, 82, 83, 92 or 93 (excluding Sub-Major
  Group 932), Minor Group 6320, or Unit Group 62200.
- The experience must be paid, and obtained in a country where you were
  qualified to practise the trade.
- Either a valid job offer of full-time work for at least 1 year, or a
  certificate of qualification in the trade issued by a Canadian provincial,
  territorial or federal authority.
- Language: CLB 5 in speaking and listening, CLB 4 in reading and writing.

There is no education requirement for this program. Education still earns CRS
points once you are in the pool, so a credential is worth declaring even though
it is not needed to qualify.
