The Ontario Workforce Priority stream is the Ontario Immigrant Nominee
Program (OINP) stream for foreign workers who hold a job offer from an
Ontario employer. Since June 2026 it is the program's only stream for skilled
workers, having replaced the eight streams Ontario ran previously.

The job offer must be for a full-time, permanent position in Ontario and must
meet the required wage level for the location of the job. Self-employed
physicians are eligible without a job offer if they hold an OHIP billing
number and certification from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of
Ontario. Applicants must intend to live and work in Ontario after permanent
residence is granted, and anyone already in Canada must hold legal status
through a work permit, study permit, or visitor record.

The nomination route depends on the NOC TEER category of the occupation. An
applicant in TEER 0, 1, 2, or 3 may indicate in the application that they want
to be nominated through the IRCC Express Entry system. That is an enhanced
nomination worth 600 CRS points, which in practice guarantees an invitation to
apply. It requires a valid Express Entry profile maintained throughout the
application period — if the profile lapses after nomination, the OINP process
must start over. An applicant in TEER 4 or 5 receives a base nomination only:
outside Express Entry, worth no CRS points.
