Express Entry manages three programs. You enter one pool, but you must be
eligible for at least one of them to be in it at all.

- Federal Skilled Worker: 1 year of TEER 0–3 experience in the last 10 years,
  anywhere in the world. CLB 7 first language. Requires a credential and, for
  foreign education, an educational credential assessment. Also requires 67 of
  100 on the selection-factor grid.
- Federal Skilled Trades: 2 years in a skilled trade in the last 5 years, plus
  either a qualifying job offer or a certificate of qualification. CLB 5
  speaking and listening, CLB 4 reading and writing. No education requirement.
- Canadian Experience Class: 1 year of skilled work in Canada in the last 3
  years, done under temporary resident status with authorization to work. CLB 7
  for TEER 0 or 1 jobs, CLB 5 for TEER 2 or 3. No education requirement.

The differences that trip people up are the look-back windows (10 years, 5
years, 3 years) and the language floors, which are not the same number across
the three programs.
